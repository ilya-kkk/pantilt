# BOM

## Printed parts
- 1x Base: four-leg printed base, central pan bearing housing area
- 1x Pan motor mount: slotted +/-5 mm tension adjustment for NEMA17
- 1x Pan output hub: bearing-supported rotating hub, cable bore through center
- 1x Pan driven pulley: integrated printed GT2-10, 80T
- 1x Rotating upper plate: carries tilt frame and tilt motor
- 1x Left tilt support: 608 bearing pocket with printed retainer geometry
- 1x Right tilt support: 608 bearing pocket plus tilt motor bracket
- 1x Tilt motor bracket: slotted NEMA17 mount, vertical belt adjustment
- 1x Tilt driven pulley: printed GT2-10, 80T, bolted to camera cradle/axle hub
- 1x Tilt camera cradle / camera plate: 160x80x5 plate, 1/4-20 longitudinal slot
- 1x Cable guides / tie slots: slots on camera plate and center cable passage

## Bought components
- 2x NEMA17 stepper motor: 42 mm frame, 31 mm hole spacing
- 2x GT2 20T metal motor pulley, 5 mm bore, 10 mm belt: one per motor
- 2x GT2 belt, 10 mm width: closed-loop length selected after final center distance
- 4x 608ZZ bearing 8x22x7: two for pan, two for tilt
- 2x 8 mm metal shaft / shoulder bolt: pan fixed shaft and tilt axle
- 8x M3 screws: NEMA17 mounting
- 8x M5 screws / washers / nuts: base, yoke, retainers, camera plate accessories
- 1x 1/4-20 UNC camera screw: camera mounting slot
- 4x Rubber feet: base legs
- 1x Panasonic HC-V760 placeholder: 65x73x139 envelope, design load 0.6 kg

## Drive ratios
- Pan: 80T / 20T = 4.0:1
- Tilt: 80T / 20T = 4.0:1
