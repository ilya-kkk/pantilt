# BOM V8

## Printed concept parts
- 1x three-leg lower base with integral 51107/608 seats and downward-shaft pan motor mount
- 1x one-piece pan rotating solid: 152 mm square-topped platform with both pan yokes (LCD-clearance rear-routed left support, direct-NEMA17 right support), both gussets, plastic journal, thrust hub, connector skirt, and real 120T GT2/2MR pulley — one printed part
- 2x 608 bearing caps
- 1x one-piece printed tilt cradle with a 39x98 mm camera saddle, both cheeks, two 7.9 mm trunnions, and integral inboard 80T GT2/2MR pulley with a 10 mm face

## Bought components
- 1x NEMA17 stepper motor for pan
- 1x StepperOnline 17HS19-2004S1 NEMA17 for tilt, 42x42x48 mm with a 5x24 mm shaft
- 1x 51107 / 8107 thrust bearing, 35x52x12 mm
- 3x 608-2RS bearing, 8x22x7 mm: two tilt and one pan radial bearing
- 1x GT2 20T metal pulley for pan; the 120T driven pulley is integral to the printed pan platform
- 1x GT2 20T metal pulley for tilt; the 80T driven pulley is integral to the printed cradle
- 1x GT2 10 mm belt, approximately 350 mm for pan; confirm after setting motor position
- 1x GT2 10 mm belt, nominal 280 mm for tilt
- 1x 1/4-20 camera screw
- M3 motor screws, M4 bearing-cap screws, M5 base fasteners
- 3x rubber feet

## Ratios
- Pan: 120T / 20T = 6.0:1
- Tilt: 80T / 20T = 4.0:1
